Fusion Lobby Browser is a website dedicated to help BONELAB players. BONELAB does not have official multiplayer support, though a mod exists that adds support for it. The website's purpose is to allow players to quickly view the current lobbies in LabFusion (the multiplayer mod), while providing many features to make the browsing experience.

The backend does not have access to any information regarding unlisted/private mods to which it not has been granted access to. If you'd like for your avatar/level to have the thumbnail displayed, send me on Discord (@hahoos) a link to the preview or (if thats even possible to do) add FusionLobbyBrowser to have preview access

It's an issue that will need to be looked into, though Google has not provided any specific reason, rather a general one which implies that they do not deem the website to be worthy of being displayed.

The purpose of the website is to allow for easier browsing of lobbies, which are sourced from services like Steam and Epic Games Online Services. I have no way of preventing such lobbies from being created. I deem to Hood RP to be "acceptable" to be displayed, though it is questionable to say the least and may be changed in the future. Currently lobbies that try to recreate real life tragedies, such as school shootings, assasinations etc. (known as True Crime Community, or TCC in short) and other less or more questionable (such as lobbies called for example "Abusive Dad RP", yes there were lobbies like that and others) are hidden from the website with no option of displaying them.

The range of lobbies hidden is subject to change and will be updated when needed
